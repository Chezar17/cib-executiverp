Debrief Of Incident

Dr. Eddy Monteval, a forensic resident at SSMC, compounded an experimental hospital drug he named “Adamantium” (health +28, immunity +42, stress +30) and gave one sample to Jajang, a North gang affiliate working with Russia on Cayo Perico. Eddy admits meeting Jajang three times, buying and reselling “join,” and negotiating a trade of drugs (settled at 20 bottles, with Jajang separately requesting 100) in exchange for Cayo information, citing a need for extra income and a wish to clear his name. He never reported the deal before it collapsed when Jajang leaked it.

Eddy was then questioned by Detectives Aiden and Tarsha, who asked whether he was CIB and who held lab access. He answered no and “nobody,” and refused their request for the recipe. Dr. Ryu Ji Kenedy, Head of Forensics, testified that he knew of only one transaction meeting and pulled Eddy’s key and access once Eddy reported his actions, which Eddy contradicts.

07/09/2026

Meeting 1: The “join” purchase

Eddy called Jajang first to ask about “join.” Jajang asked whether Eddy was sent by Commander Diraj or was CIB; Eddy joked in response, which raised suspicion. Eddy bought 2 join at $500 each, used one, and the other was damaged. He planned to resell in bulk but couldn’t, because Jajang couldn’t go to Cayo. Jajang offered that Eddy could sell stone to him. Eddy says he had already made Adamantium before this meeting.

08/09/2026 (as filed: 08/09/2027)

Meeting 2: The Adamantium sample and info trade

Eddy brought up the drug first, saying the hospital was researching drugs, and gave Jajang one sample of Adamantium. Jajang was enthusiastic and wanted 100 bottles to strengthen himself. Eddy asked for Cayo information in return. Jajang asked 50 bottles for it, Eddy valued it at 10, and they settled at 20. Eddy says he then intended to tell Commander Diraj.

13/09/2026

Meeting 3: The collapsed deal

Jajang asked for the 100 bottles and Eddy asked for a blueprint in return. The exchange never happened because Jajang leaked the deal. Eddy was taken in and questioned by Detectives Aiden and Tarsha, who offered help if he revealed the recipe; he refused. Dr. Benben was swapped in for him at that point.

Reasons for suspicion

  1. Repeated contact with a gang-linked individual. Eddy met Jajang three times, initiating contact by calling him about “join,” and admits he intended to resell what he bought.
  2. Transfer of hospital research to an outsider. At the second meeting Eddy told Jajang the hospital was researching the drug and handed over a sample of confidential work.
  3. Trading drugs for information. Eddy negotiated bottles of Adamantium for Cayo intel, pricing it at 10 and settling at 20, while Jajang separately asked for 100.
  4. Personal motive. Eddy admits he needed extra income, which weakens his claim that he only wanted to clear his name.
  5. Failure to report. He meant to tell Commander Diraj after meeting 2 but never did, and went on to a third meeting.
  6. Contradictions with Dr. Ryu Ji. The two differ on whether Eddy reported his actions, whether he held a key or access, and how many meetings took place.
  7. Evasive answers under questioning. Eddy said nobody held lab access, which is untrue given the three key holders Ryu Ji named. He also refused to give the recipe.

Interrogation summary

Role. Forensic resident under Dr. Ryu Ji, stationed at SSMC, about two months in forensics.

Lab access. The lab already existed when he returned from leave. He says he got no access once it was set up and never borrowed a key; only a locker shared with Ryu Ji held the ingredients.

The drug. Adamantium was his 4th of 9 results. Ingredients: tomato, coffee, ibuprofen, disulfiram and loperamide. He says he gave only one sample, to Jajang.

Motive. “I needed extra income. I tried to trade information for drugs to atone for my mistake, but it pulled me in deeper.”

Release. After the leak, Det. Noel rescued him. He was traded for three doctors at Principal Bank and told to run out.

Investigator’s note. Eddy’s statements confirm the sample, the negotiation and the information exchange. They conflict with Dr. Ryu Ji’s testimony on four points: key or access, number of meetings, whether the 100 bottles came from the 10-bottle offer, and whether Eddy reported his actions.

Closure

Summary of investigation

This investigation concerns the transfer of confidential hospital drug research from SSMC to Jajang, a North gang affiliate working with Russia on Cayo Perico. The SSMC drug lab was initially unlocked, and many staff handled the equipment and made their own compounds. Eddy admits the sample, the negotiation and the information exchange. Ryu Ji is treated as a witness, not a suspect, though his supervision of Eddy and his storing of the lab key in a locker shared only with Eddy raise questions about key control.

The drug is now known outside the hospital. Still to be done: resolve the key and access conflict, confirm the number of meetings and bottles through records and witness statements, clarify Ryu Ji’s motel account, and identify who is now asking for the drug.

Suspect identification and culpability

Eddy is identified through his own statements, which place him in three meetings with Jajang. His culpability is high because the exchange was deliberate and repeated, he raised the hospital’s research and the drug himself, and he had a financial motive by his own admission. His claim that he was trying to clear his name is weakened by his failure to report the deal to Commander Diraj. His denial of ever holding a key conflicts with Ryu Ji’s account, so his role may be larger than he admits. There is no evidence Ryu Ji took part in the deal. Eddy is held responsible for the transfer of information to Jajang and is charged with Espionage.

Final disposition

Eddy Monteval is held on the Espionage charge; the wider case stays open pending verification. The conflicts between the two testimonies should be checked against phone records, CCTV, and statements from Ismail, Aryn, Det. Noel and Dr. Gio. Ryu Ji’s key storage and the lab’s early lack of access control are referred to hospital and department leadership for review. Monitoring of North gang and Russia-linked activity around Cayo Perico continues.
